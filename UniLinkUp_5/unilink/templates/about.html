About UniLinkUp

Today, students, colleges and companies work in silos — with no shared, honest picture of who is actually job-ready. UniLinkUp closes that gap.

The Problem
  • Skill gaps go unnoticed until interviews.
  • Silent rejections with zero feedback.
  • Disconnected ecosystem between colleges, students and recruiters.
  • No early exposure for 1st/2nd year students.
Our Solution
  • Smart skill & job matching with a live match percentage.
  • Real interview & aptitude practice with instant scoring.
  • Feedback that helps every rejection become a next step.
  • Short-term paid internships for early, real-world experience.
